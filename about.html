Our Story & Mission

Fresh Mobile Detailing was built from a genuine love for clean cars and an absolute obsession with doing things right. Starting out in Richmond, TX, we built our reputation as a premier mobile auto detailing service one vehicle at a time. Whether we are performing a meticulous hand wash on a daily commuter, executing a multi-stage paint correction on a luxury SUV, or applying a flawless ceramic coating to an exotic sports car, we deliver the same elite-level attention to detail.

We understand that your vehicle is a significant investment. That is why we bring the professional car wash and detailing studio directly to your driveway. Every client we serve across Richmond, Katy, Sugar Land, and the Greater Houston area is a relationship, not a transaction. Our growth has been entirely word of mouth, and the mirror-like reflections on our clients' cars speak for themselves.

The Mobile Detailing Difference

Exterior Restoration & Paint Correction

We go far beyond a standard car wash. Our exterior detailing packages include safe hand-washing techniques, iron decontamination, clay bar treatments, and advanced paint correction to permanently remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation, revealing a flawless, factory-fresh clear coat.

Deep Interior Rejuvenation

A clean cabin transforms your driving experience. Our interior detailing services feature hot water extraction for carpets and upholstery, steam cleaning to kill bacteria, and premium leather conditioning to restore suppleness and protect against UV cracking and wear.

Premium Convenience

We are a fully equipped, 100% mobile detailing unit. We arrive at your home, office, or apartment complex with our own spot-free water supply and electricity. You get unparalleled auto detailing results without ever having to wait in line at a car wash or drop your vehicle off at a shop.
